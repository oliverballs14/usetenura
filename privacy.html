Your privacy

Tenura is pre-launch. This page explains what we collect from this website and why.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you enter. We don't collect anything else through the form, and we don't ask for payment details or tenant data on this website.

Why we collect it

To contact you about Tenura's early access and pilots. We won't use your email for anything unrelated, and we won't sell or share it for marketing.

Who handles it

Waitlist emails are processed by the form provider we use to receive signups, and stored so we can get in touch. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may also log standard technical data such as IP addresses when you visit the site.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove it, or until the waitlist is no longer needed, whichever comes first.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email hello@usetenura.com and we'll action it.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies or use tracking or advertising tools.

Last updated October 2026. If this changes before launch, we'll update this page.
