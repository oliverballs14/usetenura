Ongoing visibility,
not a snapshot

Tenura tracks and verifies rent payment behaviour across the whole tenancy — then turns it into a clear, reliable profile that any landlord can act on.

How it works

Four steps from
setup to insight

01

Landlord or agent registers their property

You sign up, add your property, and invite your tenant. The setup takes under five minutes and requires no technical knowledge. We handle the rest.

Letting agents can onboard their entire portfolio at once — Tenura is built for scale from day one. A single dashboard covers every property you manage.

Who does this
Landlords and letting agents. This is the only step that requires active setup — everything after is automatic.
What you'll need
Your property address and your tenant's email address. That's it. No paperwork, no integrations to configure.
02

Tenant connects their bank account

Your tenant receives an invitation and connects their bank account via Open Banking. This is a read-only connection — we can see transaction data, but we cannot move money or access account details beyond what's needed.

Tenants who don't want to use Open Banking can use manual logging instead — but Open Banking provides verified data, while manual entries are self-reported and flagged accordingly in any landlord-facing report.

Open Banking
Regulated under FCA's PSD2 framework. Read-only access, encrypted in transit, and revocable by the tenant at any time.
Tenant consent
Tenants explicitly consent to what data is shared and with whom. They can revoke access or export their own data at any time.
03

We track and verify payments automatically

Once connected, Tenura monitors rent payments in real time. Every payment is timestamped, matched to the agreed rent amount and due date, and stored in a verified record that neither landlord nor tenant can edit retroactively.

We track on-time percentage, patterns across months, missed payments, late payments, and partial payments — every dimension of payment behaviour that matters to a future landlord.

What we track
On-time payments, late payments (with days late), missed payments, partial payments, and patterns over time. All timestamped and locked.
Tamper-proof
Records are independently verified via bank data — neither party can retroactively alter the payment history. What happened, happened.
04

A verified profile is generated and becomes portable

As payments accumulate, a reliability score is generated — a clear, honest summary of how this tenant actually pays. When they move to their next property, the profile moves with them. Shareable with any future landlord as verified proof.

For landlords screening new tenants, a Tenura profile is the strongest signal available — because it reflects real behaviour across real tenancies, not a single checked box on a move-in form.

For screening
Landlords can request a Tenura profile from prospective tenants before a viewing — or at offer stage as part of due diligence.
For tenants
A strong Tenura profile is competitive evidence — particularly for tenants without a long rental history, or who are renting in a competitive market.

The data

Every dimension of
payment behaviour

On-time payment percentage

What percentage of rent payments were made by the due date, calculated across the full tenancy. The single most predictive signal for future behaviour.

Average days late (when late)

There's a difference between 2 days late and 14 days late. We track this — and it matters. Patterns of marginal lateness look very different from habitual near-default.

Missed payment history

Total missed payments, when they occurred in the tenancy, and what happened next. Context matters — a missed payment in month 1 is different from one in month 18.

Payment trend over time

Is the tenant getting better or worse? A declining trend in the last six months of a tenancy is a signal that a static on-time percentage would never surface.

Number of tracked tenancies

One clean record is good. Three is much better. The more tenancies in the profile, the more reliable the signal — and we show you exactly how many you're looking at.

Verification

How we make sure
the data is real

Unverified self-reported data is the old way. Tenura verifies independently — or makes clear when something can't be verified, so you can weight it accordingly.

🏦

Open Banking (primary)

Direct, read-only access to the tenant's bank transaction data via FCA-regulated Open Banking APIs. Payments are confirmed by the bank itself — not by the tenant's say-so.

Highest confidence
📋

Landlord-confirmed logging

Where a tenant doesn't connect via Open Banking, landlords can log payments directly. These entries are visible in the profile and clearly marked as landlord-confirmed rather than bank-verified.

Medium confidence
📄

Document cross-reference

For historical tenancies without Open Banking data, tenants can upload bank statements which are cross-referenced against claimed payment dates. Flagged clearly in the profile.

Privacy & compliance

Built on consent,
not extraction

We're collecting sensitive financial data. We take that seriously — both legally and as a matter of design.

🔒

GDPR-compliant by design

Data is collected with explicit consent, stored in the UK, and subject to full subject access and deletion rights. You can export or delete your data at any time.

🏦

FCA-regulated Open Banking

Open Banking in the UK operates under FCA regulation and the Payment Services Directive 2. Our banking partners are fully authorised and regulated.

🔑

Read-only bank access

We can see transactions. We cannot move money, access savings or other accounts, or perform any action on the tenant's behalf. Read-only, always.

✓

Tenant controls what's shared

Tenants decide which landlords can view their profile, for how long, and can revoke access immediately. No data leaves without tenant authorisation.
